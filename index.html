Frequently Asked Questions

For Salaried ITR: Form 16 from employer, Bank Statement (April to March), Aadhaar card, PAN card, investment proofs (LIC, PPF, Mutual Funds u/s 80C). For Business ITR: Sales/Purchase figures, bank statements, and stock details. We match everything with your Form 26AS, AIS, and TIS statements.

Under Section 44AB of the Income Tax Act, Tax Audit is mandatory if turnover exceeds ₹1 Crore for business (or ₹10 Crore if digital transactions exceed 95%). For professionals under Sec 44ADA, the limit is ₹50 Lakhs (or ₹75 Lakhs under digital limits). Form 3CD audit report must be uploaded by the specified due date.

We perform line-item matching between your Purchase Register and GSTR-2B generated on the GST portal. Unmatched invoices are flagged to your vendors so that maximum legitimate Input Tax Credit (ITC) is claimed safely in GSTR-3B without receiving portal mismatch notices.
